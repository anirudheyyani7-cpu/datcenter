# Brookfield India DC Investment Strategy

[[Brookfield Asset Management]] is pursuing an aggressive [[India]] data center investment strategy with a $500M capital commitment targeting 10x returns.

## Investment Parameters

- **Capital**: $500M deployment
- **Target Returns**: 10x multiple (implying $5B total value creation)
- **Geographic Focus**: [[India]]
- **Market Context**: [[India DC Market]] projected to grow from $10Bn (2025) to $22Bn (2030)
- **Investor Profile**: [[Institutional Investor]] with [[Infrastructure Asset]] expertise

## Immediate Priorities

1. **[[Power Allocation]]**: Secure 150MW across 2-3 [[Tier-1 Metro Sites]]
2. **Lifecycle Positioning**: Determine optimal entry point in [[DC Development Lifecycle]]
3. **Market Timing**: Capitalize on India's [[Hyperscale Cloud]] expansion and [[AI Infrastructure]] growth

## Strategic Position

Brookfield's primary competitive focus is launching a bid for [[Carlyle Group]]'s 24% stake in [[Nxtra Data]], positioning them as a major player in India's rapidly expanding hyperscale market.

## DC Lifecycle Investment Question

The core strategic question: Which phase of the [[Data Center Lifecycle]] maximizes IRR potential for this capital deployment?

### Lifecycle Options:
1. [[Greenfield Development]] - land + construction, highest risk, highest control
2. [[Brownfield Expansion]] - operating assets, moderate risk, faster deployment
3. [[Operating Asset Acquisition]] - lower risk, immediate cash flow
4. [[Joint Venture Structures]] - shared risk, market access with local operators
5. [[Power Infrastructure]] - substations, fiber development

## Market Intelligence

[[India]] presents unique opportunities across the [[Tier-3 Cities]] expansion narrative and [[Hyperscale DC]] demand from global cloud providers establishing regional presence.

Brookfield's $10Bn funnel by 2025 suggests they're evaluating multiple asset types simultaneously, but the [[Nxtra Data]] bid indicates preference for established platforms with expansion potential.
