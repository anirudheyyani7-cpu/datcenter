# Brookfield Nxtra Data Competitive Bid

[[Brookfield Asset Management]] is preparing a competitive acquisition bid for [[Carlyle Group]]'s 24% minority stake in [[Nxtra Data]], India's leading colocation and hyperscale data center platform.

## Deal Structure

- **Target Asset**: 24% stake in [[Nxtra Data]]
- **Current Owner**: [[Carlyle Group]]
- **Strategic Rationale**: Platform acquisition for [[India DC Market]] expansion
- **Priority Level**: Top priority for Brookfield's India infrastructure team

## Nxtra Data Context

[[Nxtra Data]] is the data center arm of [[Bharti Airtel]], operating multiple facilities across India's metro and [[Tier-2 Cities]]. The platform provides:

- Established customer relationships with [[Hyperscale Operators]]
- Existing land bank for [[Greenfield Development]]
- Operational expertise in [[India Regulatory Environment]]
- Revenue-generating assets (vs. pure development risk)

## Competitive Dynamics

This bid positions Brookfield against other infrastructure funds competing for India DC exposure. The 24% minority stake offers:

1. Partnership with telecom infrastructure leader ([[Bharti Airtel]])
2. Access to expansion pipeline without full development risk
3. Platform for additional bolt-on acquisitions
4. Potential path to majority control over time

## Investment Thesis Alignment

The [[Nxtra Data]] bid aligns with Brookfield's $500M India deployment by providing a [[Hybrid Investment Strategy]] - acquiring cash-flowing assets with embedded growth options.
