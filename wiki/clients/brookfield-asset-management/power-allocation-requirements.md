# Brookfield Power Allocation Requirements

## Target Capacity

[[Brookfield Asset Management]] has established a clear power procurement mandate: **150MW across 2-3 [[Tier-1 Metro Sites]]** in India.

## Strategic Rationale

This multi-site approach reflects several sophisticated considerations:

### Geographic Diversification
- Spreading 150MW across 2-3 locations (50-75MW per site)
- Reduces single-market exposure
- Aligns with [[Hyperscale Cloud]] provider requirements for geographic redundancy

### Market Selection
Likely targets among [[India Tier-1 Markets]]:
- [[Mumbai]] - Financial hub, subsea cable landing
- [[Chennai]] - Southern connectivity, lower costs
- [[Bangalore]] - Technology ecosystem concentration
- [[Hyderabad]] - Emerging hyperscale destination

### Power Constraints Reality

150MW represents serious institutional scale. In India's constrained power environment, this requires:

- Long-term [[Power Purchase Agreements]] with [[State DISCOMs]]
- Potential [[Captive Power]] development
- Strategic relationships with [[Power Infrastructure]] providers
- 24-36 month lead time for full allocation

## Competitive Landscape

[[Brookfield]] competes with:
- [[CtrlS]] (aggressive Tier-2/3 expansion)
- [[Yotta Infrastructure]] (Hiranandani partnership)
- [[Digital Realty]] (recent India entry)
- [[Princeton Digital Group]] (PAG Capital backing)

## Execution Complexity

Securing 150MW is **not** a simple procurement exercise:

1. **Regulatory navigation**: State-level approvals vary
2. **Grid capacity**: Existing infrastructure limitations
3. **Pricing volatility**: Power costs fluctuate significantly
4. **Timeline pressure**: Hyperscale commitments drive urgency
