# India Data Center Investment Mandate

[[Blackstone Credit]] has established a clear mandate to invest in the [[India]] [[data center]] market, representing a significant strategic commitment to one of the world's fastest-growing digital infrastructure markets.

## Investment Focus

The firm is specifically focused on the [[Indian DC Market]], seeking opportunities that align with their [[private equity]] and infrastructure deployment capabilities. This represents a deliberate geographic and sector concentration strategy.

## Strategic Context

This mandate sits within a broader capital deployment framework estimated at **$15 billion** in PE/infrastructure capital targeting India data centers by 2030, suggesting [[Blackstone Credit]] is positioning for meaningful market share in this growth trajectory.

## Deal Sourcing Approach

The firm's top priority is launching a [[Proprietary Deal Sourcing]] process, specifically targeting:
- [[Yotta]] minority stake opportunities
- [[STT GDC India]] minority stake positions

This focus on [[minority stakes]] in established platform operators suggests a strategy of partnering with proven developers rather than greenfield development or majority control acquisitions.

## Market Timing

The India-focused mandate comes at a time of explosive growth in Indian digital infrastructure, driven by [[hyperscale]] demand, [[AI workloads]], and sovereign data localization requirements. The firm's investor profile indicates they are seeking established platforms with operating history rather than early-stage development risk.
