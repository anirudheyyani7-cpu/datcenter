# Target Portfolio Companies - India Focus

[[Blackstone Credit]] has identified specific target companies for minority stake investments in the [[India DC Market]], reflecting a highly targeted approach to market entry.

## Primary Targets

### Yotta Infrastructure

[[Yotta]] represents a key target for [[minority stake]] investment. Yotta is India's largest Tier-4 data center platform, with facilities in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Chennai. The company has been expanding rapidly with hyperscale capacity and has attracted significant institutional capital.

**Strategic Rationale:**
- Proven hyperscale platform
- Multiple operational facilities
- Strong management team
- Established customer relationships with cloud providers
- Tier-4 certification providing competitive moat

### STT GDC India

[[STT GDC India]], part of the Singapore-based [[ST Telemedia Global Data Centres]], represents another priority target for minority investment.

**Strategic Rationale:**
- Regional platform with parent company expertise
- Established presence across key Indian metros
- Access to STT's global customer relationships
- Proven operational track record
- Strong ESG and sustainability credentials

## Investment Structure

The focus on [[minority stakes]] rather than control positions suggests:
- Partnership approach with existing management
- Lower entry price points than majority acquisitions
- Ability to diversify across multiple platforms
- Access to operational expertise without operational burden

## Deal Sourcing Strategy

The launch of a [[Proprietary Deal Sourcing]] process indicates [[Blackstone Credit]] is building direct relationships with these platforms rather than waiting for marketed processes, potentially providing:
- Better pricing and terms
- First-mover advantage on future opportunities
- Deeper due diligence access
- Customized deal structures
