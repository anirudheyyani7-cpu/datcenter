# Blackstone Credit Deployment Targets

[[Blackstone Credit]] has communicated substantial capital deployment goals that signal significant appetite for [[India Data Center Investment]] opportunities.

## Capital Deployment Funnel

**$15 Billion by 2030**: The firm has a funnel size of $15Bn targeting PE/Infrastructure capital deployment by 2030. This represents a major capital pool seeking deployment in the [[Private Equity]] and [[Infrastructure Investment]] space, with specific focus on [[India Data Center Market]].

### Deployment Timeframe

With approximately 5-6 years to deploy $15Bn (assuming current date ~2024-2025), this implies:
- Annual deployment target: ~$2.5-3Bn
- Need for multiple large-scale transactions
- Focus on [[Platform Investments]] and [[Secondary Transactions]]

## Priority Transactions

**Top Priority: Nxtra Secondary Stake**: The firm has identified the [[Nxtra Secondary Stake Auction]] as their top priority, indicating:
- Active pursuit of operating platform acquisitions
- Interest in established [[Data Center Operators]] with proven track records
- Willingness to participate in competitive auction processes
- Focus on [[Tier-1 Assets]] with scale

## Strategic Positioning

The size of the deployment funnel positions Blackstone Credit as a major force in the [[India DC Ecosystem]]. With $15Bn to deploy, they can:
- Acquire controlling stakes in major platforms
- Fund [[Greenfield Development]] at scale
- Participate in multiple [[Joint Ventures]]
- Support [[Build-to-Suit]] commitments for hyperscalers
