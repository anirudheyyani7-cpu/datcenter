# Mytrah Group — Data Centre Entry Strategy

## Client Profile

**Mytrah Group** is a renewable energy company with established expertise in **solar EPC** (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) seeking to diversify into the **data centre business**. This represents a strategic pivot from their core solar infrastructure background into a new sector where they have no prior experience.

## Background & Core Competencies

- **Primary Expertise**: Solar EPC and renewable energy infrastructure
- **Capital Position**: Strong financial backing available for expansion into new sectors
- **Project Delivery Experience**: Proven track record in large-scale infrastructure development and project execution
- **Data Centre Experience**: None — entirely new vertical, actively seeking knowledge transfer and partnership

## Strategic Objectives

### Target Market Segment

Mytrah is pursuing a **300 MW [[Tier-3]] [[Hyperscale Data Centre]]** development — a significant market entry that aligns with [[Greenfield Data Centre Development]] best practices:

- **Capacity Target**: 300 MW [[Hyperscale Data Centre]]
- **Infrastructure Tier**: [[Tier-3]] specifications
- **Market Positioning**: Enterprise hyperscale segment

### Readiness Assessment

**Current Readiness Score**: 72/100

This moderate-to-high readiness score reflects:
- ✅ Strong financial position and available capital
- ✅ Infrastructure development experience from solar projects
- ✅ Proven large-scale project execution capabilities
- ⚠️ Zero data centre domain expertise — requires comprehensive knowledge bridging

The company recognizes this gap and is actively seeking to bridge it through partnerships and consulting engagement.

## Engagement Requirements

### Recommended Infrastructure Approach

**Primary Recommendation**: [[Joint Venture]] (JV) structure

Given Mytrah's limited sector experience but strong execution capabilities, a JV arrangement offers optimal risk mitigation while leveraging their capital and EPC expertise.

### Consulting Scope Requirements

The client requires **end-to-end consulting support** covering:

1. **Market Education**: [[Data Centre Fundamentals]] and sector orientation
2. **Partnership Strategy**: [[Partnership Strategy]] development and partner identification
3. **Technical Architecture**: Design and specifications for [[Tier-3]] requirements
4. **Regulatory Navigation**: Compliance and regulatory framework guidance
5. **Operational Readiness**: Planning and preparation for operations
