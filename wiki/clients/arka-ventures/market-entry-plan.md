# Arka Ventures - Data Center Market Entry Plan

## Executive Request
Client has requested comprehensive market entry plan for [[Data Center Market]] with focus on securing [[Hyperscaler BTS Partnership]].

## Market Entry Objectives

### Primary Goal
Secure 60-80 MW [[Build-to-Suit Agreement]] with tier-1 [[Hyperscaler]] in [[India Data Center Market]].

### Success Metrics
- Signed BTS agreement within 12-18 months
- Pre-committed capacity: 60-80 MW
- Long-term lease: 15+ years typical
- Position for $7.5Bn market opportunity by 2035

## Recommended Entry Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation Building (Months 1-6)
1. **Capability Development**
   - Establish [[DC Development]] team
   - Partner with experienced [[EPC Contractor]]
   - Secure [[Tier-3 Certification]] readiness
   - Build [[Power Infrastructure]] sourcing capability

2. **Site Strategy**
   - Identify 2-3 [[Greenfield Sites]] in key metros
   - Focus on [[Mumbai]], [[Chennai]], [[Hyderabad]]
   - Ensure 60-80 MW power availability
   - Proximity to [[Fiber Connectivity]] infrastructure

3. **Financial Structuring**
   - Prepare [[Development Capital]] plan
   - Structure [[Joint Venture]] or partnership options
   - Explore [[Infrastructure Fund]] backing

### Phase 2: Hyperscaler Engagement (Months 4-12)
1. **Target Identification**
   - [[AWS India]] expansion plans
   - [[Microsoft Azure]] availability zone needs
   - [[Google Cloud]] regional growth
   - [[Oracle Cloud]] emerging requirements

2. **Engagement Approach**
   - Leverage [[RFP Response]] expertise
   - Present [[Greenfield]] site options
   - Demonstrate [[Power Infrastructure]] security
   - Show [[Tier-3]] technical capability

### Phase 3: Deal Execution (Months 9-18)
1. **Negotiation Framework**
   - [[Build-to-Suit Agreement]] terms
   - [[Power Purchase Agreement]] structure
   - [[Service Level Agreement]] commitments
   - Delivery timeline guarantees

## Critical Success Factors
- Speed to market with site control
- Credible technical partnership
- Secured power allocation
- Competitive pricing structure
