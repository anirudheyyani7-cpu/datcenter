# Entry Strategy Requirements

**Client:** [[Blackstone Capital]]
**Market:** [[India Data Center Market]]
**Timeline:** Term sheet phase (immediate)

## Core Question

"What are my possible entry points into the Indian DC market?"

This indicates the client is evaluating **multiple acquisition and partnership pathways** rather than committing to a single approach.

## Strategic Dimensions to Address

### 1. Acquisition Target Types
- Tier-3 city operators with expansion potential
- Metro-focused operators in [[Mumbai]], [[Delhi NCR]], [[Bangalore]]
- Vertically integrated players (construction + operations)
- Pure-play operators vs. real estate developers

### 2. Transaction Structures
- Majority stake acquisition ([[Platform Acquisition]])
- Minority investment with growth capital
- [[Joint Venture]] with local developer or operator
- Carve-out from larger conglomerate

### 3. Geographic Strategy
- Pan-India platform vs. regional focus
- Metro markets ([[Tier-1 Cities]]) vs. emerging markets ([[Tier-2 Cities]], [[Tier-3 Cities]])
- Hyperscale-ready vs. retail/enterprise colocation

### 4. Operational Model
- Acquire operating platform vs. development pipeline
- Buy-and-build strategy
- [[Build-Operate-Transfer]] considerations

## Next Steps

Blackstone requires:
1. Competitive landscape mapping
2. Target screening and prioritization
3. Preliminary valuation frameworks
4. Partnership structure options
