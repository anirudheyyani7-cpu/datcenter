# Blackstone Capital

**Profile:** New client seeking market entry
**Geography:** [[India Data Center Market]]
**Stage:** Platform acquisition planning

## Strategic Context

Blackstone Capital is exploring entry into the [[India Data Center Market]], which represents a **$6.4B opportunity by 2030**. The firm is in the target identification and term sheet phase, indicating readiness to deploy capital for a [[Platform Acquisition]].

## Top Priority

**Execute Platform Acquisition** — The client requires:
- Target identification across Indian data center operators
- Term sheet preparation and negotiation support
- Market positioning analysis

This suggests a preference for [[Brownfield Investment]] or strategic [[Joint Venture]] with an established operator rather than [[Greenfield Development]].

## Market Entry Questions

Blackstone has explicitly requested analysis of **entry point options** in the Indian DC market, indicating evaluation of multiple pathways:
- Direct acquisition of existing operators
- Partnership structures with local players
- Build-operate-transfer models
- Regional vs. pan-India strategies
