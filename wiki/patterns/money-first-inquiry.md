# Money-First Inquiry Pattern

## Pattern Description

Client opens engagement with direct question about maximizing returns: "Where should I go to make a lot of money?"

## Pattern Indicators

### Typical Characteristics
- New to data center sector
- Limited technical understanding
- Revenue-focused without risk appreciation
- Underestimate capital requirements
- Unclear on market dynamics

### Example: Arka Ventures
**Query:** "Tell me where should I go to make a lot of money"
**Profile:** New entrant, no DC experience
**Readiness:** 42/100 (low)
**Recommended Strategy:** [[Joint Venture]]

## Response Framework

### Phase 1: Reality Check
- Data center economics overview
- Capital intensity discussion ($10-15M per MW)
- Timeline expectations (24-36 months to revenue)
- Risk profile assessment

### Phase 2: Market Education
- [[Tier-1 Markets]] vs [[Tier-2 Markets]] vs [[Tier-3 Markets]]
- [[Supply-Demand Dynamics]]
- [[Hyperscale]] vs [[Enterprise]] vs [[Edge]] segments
- [[Power Availability]] constraints

### Phase 3: Strategic Positioning
- Risk-return spectrum mapping
- Partnership models ([[Joint Venture]], development agreements)
- Market entry sequencing
- Capital efficiency strategies

## Common Outcomes

### Path A: Recalibration
Client adjusts expectations, pursues partnership model, focuses on learning

### Path B: Capital Deployment
Client has sufficient resources, proceeds with guided market entry

### Path C: Exit
Client realizes mismatch between expectations and reality, exits sector

## Best Practices
- Lead with education, not sales
- Quantify capital and time requirements early
- Assess true risk tolerance
- Provide realistic return expectations
- Offer structured entry pathways
