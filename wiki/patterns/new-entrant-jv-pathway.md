# New Entrant JV Pathway Pattern

## Pattern Overview

A recurring strategic pathway for **new datacenter market entrants** with moderate readiness scores (60-79) seeking to minimize risk while gaining operational expertise.

## Pattern Trigger Conditions

**Client Profile**:
- New to [[datacenter sector]]
- [[Readiness score]] 60-79
- Strong capital position
- Limited operational track record
- Requesting broad "opportunity assessment"

**Example**: [[Arka Ventures]] - readiness 72, new profile, seeking cockpit view of opportunities

## Recommended Sequence

### Phase 1: Market Education & Selection (Months 1-3)
- Provide comprehensive [[datacenter sector]] landscape
- Identify 2-3 target [[Tier-2]] or [[Tier-3]] markets
- Assess competitive dynamics and supply-demand
- Map potential [[JV partners]] in target markets

### Phase 2: Partnership Development (Months 4-6)
- Shortlist established datacenter operators
- Evaluate [[JV structure]] options (equity split, governance)
- Conduct due diligence on partner capabilities
- Negotiate term sheets

### Phase 3: Deal Structuring (Months 7-9)
- Define capital contribution and equity ownership
- Establish governance framework
- Determine roles: who leads development vs. operations
- Create exit strategy alignment

### Phase 4: Execution (Months 10+)
- Joint site selection and land acquisition
- Collaborative design and engineering
- Shared vendor management
- Knowledge transfer from operating partner to investor

## Success Metrics

**Year 1**:
- Partnership agreement signed
- Site secured with power allocation
- Development plan approved

**Year 2-3**:
- Facility constructed and commissioned
- First anchor tenant signed
- 30-50% pre-lease commitment achieved

**Year 3-5**:
- 70%+ occupancy achieved
- Positive EBITDA
- Investor develops internal operational capabilities

## Risk Mitigation Elements

- **Partner vetting**: Track record in similar markets
- **Governance clarity**: Decision rights clearly defined
- **Milestone-based funding**: Capital released against achievements
- **Independent technical advisors**: Third-party validation

## Common Variations

**Build-to-Suit JV**:
- Pre-secured anchor tenant (hyperscaler or enterprise)
- Lower market risk, guaranteed initial revenue
- Partner provides development expertise

**Brownfield JV**:
- Acquire existing underperforming facility
- Partner provides turnaround expertise
- Faster time-to-revenue

## Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

- **Solo greenfield attempt** with readiness <80
- **Multi-market simultaneous entry** without track record
- **Partnering with unproven operators** to save costs
- **Vague governance structures** leading to deadlock
