# Institutional India DC Investment Playbook

A repeatable pattern for institutional investors deploying $100-500M into India's data center market with 5-7 year hold and [[REIT-exit]].

## Pattern Components

### 1. Market Entry (Year 0-1)

- **Capital Allocation**: $150-300M across 2-3 operators
- **Structure**: Mezzanine debt + equity kicker (20-30% ownership)
- **Target Operators**: 
  - Established: [[Yotta Infrastructure]], [[CtrlS]], [[Sify]]
  - Emerging: [[Adani Data Centers]], regional players

### 2. Portfolio Build (Year 1-4)

- **Deployment**: Fund 200-300MW of new capacity across Tier-1 metros
- **Geographic Mix**: 
  - 50% [[Mumbai]] (premium pricing)
  - 30% [[Chennai]]/[[Hyderabad]] (cost efficiency)
  - 20% Tier-2 (strategic hedging)
- **Customer Mix**: 60% hyperscale, 30% enterprise, 10% government

### 3. Stabilization (Year 3-5)

- **Occupancy Target**: 75-85% across portfolio
- **Revenue Metrics**: $80-120M ARR from long-term contracts
- **EBITDA Margin**: 45-55% at stabilized facilities
- **REIT Preparation**: Financial audits, governance structuring

### 4. Exit (Year 5-7)

**Option A: REIT IPO**
- List on [[NSE]]/[[BSE]] as dedicated DC REIT
- Target valuation: 12-15x EBITDA
- Investor exit via public market sale (lock-up: 6-12 months)

**Option B: Portfolio Sale**
- Sell to existing REIT ([[Embassy]], [[Mindspace]])
- Strategic buyer (Equinix, Digital Realty entering India)
- Private equity continuation fund

## Risk Mitigation

- **Regulatory**: Partner with local operators (51% Indian ownership where required)
- **Power**: Dual-source PPAs, captive solar for 20-30% of load
- **Execution**: Milestone-based capital deployment, construction guarantees
- **FX Hedging**: Borrow in ₹ to match asset/liability currency

## Expected Returns

- **Current Yield**: 6-9% from debt instruments
- **Exit Multiple**: 2.5-3.5x MOIC over 6-7 years
- **IRR Target**: 18-24% gross

## Case Study: Blackstone Credit

- **Allocation**: $200M across operators
- **Anchor Investment**: $120M into [[Yotta Infrastructure]] [[pre-SPAC-co-investment]]
- **Thesis**: Ride SPAC merger + REIT conversion dual catalyst
- **Timeline**: 24-month SPAC exit, reinvest proceeds into REIT platform
