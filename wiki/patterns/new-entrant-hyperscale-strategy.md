# New Entrant Hyperscale Market Entry Pattern

## Pattern Overview
A repeatable strategy for **new market entrants** to establish credibility and secure first [[Hyperscaler BTS Partnership]] in competitive [[Data Center Market]].

## Pattern Context
- **When to use**: New player with capital but no DC track record
- **Market conditions**: High hyperscaler demand, constrained supply
- **Critical asset**: Access to power infrastructure
- **Timeline**: 12-24 months to first deal

## Core Strategy Components

### 1. Power-First Approach
**Insight**: In constrained markets, power secures the deal

**Actions**:
- Identify 2-3 sites with 60-80 MW firm power allocations
- Secure power commitments before approaching [[Hyperscaler]]
- Consider [[Power Purchase Agreement]] pre-negotiation
- Focus on metros with [[Fiber Connectivity]] infrastructure

### 2. Credibility Through Partnership
**Insight**: Partner capabilities substitute for track record

**Actions**:
- Engage proven [[EPC Contractor]] with hyperscale references
- Structure [[Joint Venture]] with experienced [[DC Developer]]
- Secure backing from recognized [[Infrastructure Fund]]
- Hire key executives with hyperscaler relationship history

### 3. Speed-to-Market Advantage
**Insight**: Hyperscalers value execution speed over cost

**Actions**:
- Achieve site control and permitting in parallel
- Pre-qualify [[Tier-3 Certification]] pathway
- Offer accelerated delivery timelines (12-18 months)
- Demonstrate financial close capability

### 4. Targeted Hyperscaler Engagement
**Insight**: Focus beats spray-and-pray

**Actions**:
- Select 1-2 [[Cloud Service Provider]] targets based on public expansion plans
- Research specific availability zone requirements
- Engage through direct real estate team contact (not RFP)
- Present turnkey solution, not just land

## Implementation Phases

### Phase 1: Foundation (0-6 months)
1. Secure 2-3 [[Greenfield Sites]] with power
2. Structure development partnerships
3. Build technical delivery team
4. Achieve preliminary permitting

### Phase 2: Engagement (4-12 months)
1. Initiate hyperscaler discussions
2. Present site options and capabilities
3. Respond to technical requirements
4. Negotiate [[Build-to-Suit Agreement]] terms

### Phase 3: Execution (9-24 months)
1. Financial close on first deal
2. Construction and delivery
3. Achieve [[Tier-3 Certification]]
4. Operational handover

## Success Metrics
- Signed BTS agreement within 18 months
- 60-80 MW capacity commitment
- 15+ year lease term
- Platform for portfolio expansion

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid
1. **Underestimating power timeline**: Start power procurement early
2. **Overreliance on cost**: Hyperscalers value reliability over lowest price
3. **Weak technical team**: Credibility requires proven operators
4. **Multiple simultaneous pursuits**: Focus on 1-2 targets initially
5. **Insufficient capital**: Undercapitalization kills speed advantage
