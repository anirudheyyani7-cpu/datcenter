# Solar EPC to Data Centre Transition Pattern

## Pattern Context

Renewable energy companies with **solar EPC capabilities** (like [[Mytrah Group]]) increasingly view **data centres** as complementary infrastructure plays, leveraging:

1. **Project development expertise** — site acquisition, permitting, construction management
2. **Power infrastructure skills** — electrical systems, grid interconnection
3. **Access to capital** — proven ability to finance large infrastructure projects
4. **Sustainability synergy** — renewable-powered data centres align with corporate ESG goals

## Common Transition Challenges

### Knowledge Gaps
- **IT infrastructure**: Server deployment, networking, cooling for compute loads
- **Operational complexity**: 24/7 uptime requirements vs. solar's intermittent nature
- **Customer expectations**: Hyperscaler SLAs, certification requirements ([[Tier-3 Data Centre Standard]])
- **Market dynamics**: Colocation vs. wholesale vs. build-to-suit models

### Mitigation Strategies

**Partnership Models**:
- **Technical partner**: DC operator brings domain expertise
- **Joint venture**: Risk/reward sharing with experienced developer
- **End-to-end consulting**: Advisors guide through full lifecycle

**Pilot Approach**:
- Start with **smaller edge facilities** (5-20 MW) before [[Hyperscale Data Centre]]
- Co-locate solar and DC assets for integrated energy story

## Success Factors

✅ **Leverage existing strengths**: Use EPC project management discipline  
✅ **Acknowledge gaps early**: Don't underestimate operational learning curve  
✅ **Partner strategically**: Find operators with track records  
✅ **Align incentives**: Structure deals that share knowledge, not just capital  

## Example Application

[[Mytrah Group]] exemplifies this pattern:
- Strong solar EPC foundation
- 300 MW [[Greenfield Data Centre Development]] ambition
- Seeking **partnerships** and **end-to-end consulting**
- Readiness score of 72 reflects financial/development strength offset by domain inexperience
