# New Entrant to JV Partnership Pattern

## Pattern Overview

A repeatable strategic pattern where organizations with capital but no datacenter experience enter the market through [[Joint Venture]] partnerships rather than independent development.

## Pattern Trigger Conditions

- Client has zero datacenter operational experience
- Readiness score below 60
- Strong financial capacity but limited technical capability
- Risk-conscious approach to new market entry
- Timeline flexibility (willing to invest in partnership development)

## Pattern Example: [[Notion]]

**Profile**: New entrant, no DC experience, readiness score 42
**Recommendation**: JV infrastructure approach
**Rationale**: Minimize risk while building capability

## Pattern Implementation Steps

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3)
1. Conduct comprehensive industry education
2. Define strategic objectives and success criteria
3. Establish budget parameters and return expectations
4. Identify preliminary target markets

### Phase 2: Partner Selection (Months 4-7)
1. Develop partner selection criteria
2. Screen potential JV partners (operators, developers)
3. Conduct detailed due diligence on shortlist
4. Evaluate cultural and strategic alignment
5. Negotiate term sheets

### Phase 3: Structure Design (Months 8-10)
1. Negotiate JV operating agreement
2. Define governance and decision rights
3. Establish capital contribution schedule
4. Design profit distribution mechanisms
5. Create exit and buyout provisions

### Phase 4: Execution (Months 11+)
1. Close JV formation
2. Initiate site selection and acquisition
3. Begin design and permitting
4. Launch construction phase
5. Prepare for operations transition

## Success Metrics

- JV agreement signed within 12 months of inquiry
- Partner brings operational track record (5+ facilities)
- Clear path to operational capability transfer
- Balanced risk allocation between partners
- First facility operational within 24-30 months

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

❌ **Solo Development Attempt**: High failure risk for inexperienced entrants
❌ **Rushing Partner Selection**: Misaligned partnerships create long-term problems
❌ **Overambitious First Project**: Start with manageable scale
❌ **Passive Capital Provider**: Remain engaged to build internal capability

## Pattern Variations

- **Build-to-Suit with Operating Services**: Lower equity commitment
- **Management Services Agreement**: Retain ownership, outsource operations
- **Platform JV**: Multi-facility partnership from inception
