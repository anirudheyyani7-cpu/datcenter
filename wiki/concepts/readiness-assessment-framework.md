# Datacenter Investment Readiness Assessment

## Overview
The Readiness Assessment Framework evaluates a client's preparedness to enter the [[datacenter sector]], producing a 0-100 score that informs infrastructure strategy recommendations.

## Scoring Methodology

**Score Ranges**:
- **80-100**: High readiness - candidates for [[greenfield development]] or independent build
- **60-79**: Moderate readiness - recommended for [[Joint Venture (JV)]] or [[build-to-suit]]
- **40-59**: Low readiness - suggested [[colocation]] or [[leasing strategies]]
- **0-39**: Not ready - educational phase, market exploration

## Assessment Dimensions

**Capital Availability (25 points)**:
- Investment capacity for datacenter development
- Access to debt and equity financing
- Financial staying power for 18-36 month development cycles

**Operational Expertise (25 points)**:
- In-house datacenter knowledge
- Technical team capabilities
- Track record in [[critical infrastructure]]

**Market Knowledge (20 points)**:
- Understanding of [[datacenter market dynamics]]
- Geographic market selection clarity
- Competitive landscape awareness

**Strategic Clarity (15 points)**:
- Clear investment thesis
- Target customer segments identified
- Exit strategy defined

**Execution Readiness (15 points)**:
- Decision-making speed
- Organizational alignment
- Partnership appetite

## Score Interpretation: 72/100

A score of **72** (as seen with [[Arka Ventures]]) indicates:
- **Strong capital position** but operational gaps
- **Market awareness** present but needs deepening
- **Ideal candidate for JV partnership**
- Requires structured guidance on market entry

## Strategic Implications by Score

**70-79 Range**:
- Avoid independent [[greenfield]] projects initially
- Pursue partnerships with established operators
- Focus on single-market entry rather than multi-market expansion
- Invest in team building and knowledge acquisition
