# REIT Exit Strategy

A **REIT exit** involves converting a portfolio of income-generating real estate assets (like [[data centers]]) into a publicly traded Real Estate Investment Trust, providing liquidity to early investors.

## Mechanics

1. **Portfolio Aggregation**: Operator builds 150-500MW across multiple facilities
2. **Income Stabilization**: Achieve 70%+ occupancy with long-term lease contracts
3. **REIT Structuring**: Convert into tax-advantaged entity (90% of income distributed)
4. **Public Listing**: IPO on domestic exchange ([[NSE]], [[BSE]] in India) or international markets
5. **Investor Liquidity**: Early equity holders sell stake at public market valuation

## Timing

Typical timeline:

- **Year 0-3**: Build and lease-up phase
- **Year 3-5**: Stabilization, financial audits, REIT structuring
- **Year 5-7**: IPO or sale to existing REIT platform

## India-Specific Considerations

- India REIT regulations require ₹500Cr (~$60M) minimum asset value
- Must distribute 90% of net distributable cash flow
- Tax advantages: No DDT (Dividend Distribution Tax) at REIT level
- Examples: [[Embassy Office Parks REIT]], [[Mindspace Business Parks REIT]]

## Investor Advantage

- **Yield**: 6-8% annual distributions during hold period
- **Appreciation**: Asset revaluation at public market multiples (12-18x EBITDA)
- **Liquidity**: Exit via secondary market rather than bilateral sale
