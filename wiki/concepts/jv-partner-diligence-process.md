# JV Partner Diligence Process

## Overview
The JV Partner Diligence Process is a structured methodology for evaluating potential [[Joint Venture]] partners in [[Data Center Development]]. This is critical for new market entrants like [[Arka Ventures]] who need to partner with established operators.

## Key Evaluation Criteria

### Technical Capabilities
- Track record in [[Hyperscale Data Center]] delivery
- [[Power Infrastructure]] sourcing expertise
- [[Site Selection]] methodology and success rate
- Design and construction management capabilities
- [[Tier-3 Markets]] vs [[Tier-1 Markets]] experience

### Financial Strength
- Balance sheet capacity for co-investment
- Access to construction financing
- Existing relationships with [[Hyperscale Tenants]]
- Historical project returns and performance

### Operational Excellence
- Development timeline track record
- Cost control and budget management
- Regulatory approval success rate
- Utility and municipality relationships

### Strategic Alignment
- Geographic focus compatibility
- Scale ambitions and growth trajectory
- Governance and decision-making structure
- Exit strategy alignment

## Process Stages
1. **Initial Screening**: Desktop review of credentials
2. **Management Meetings**: Assess culture and capability
3. **Reference Checks**: Validate past performance with [[Hyperscale Clients]]
4. **Financial Analysis**: Deep dive on capital structure
5. **Legal Review**: Partnership agreement structure
6. **Deal Structuring**: Align economics and governance
