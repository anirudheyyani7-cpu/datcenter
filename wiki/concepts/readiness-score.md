# Readiness Score

## Definition
A quantitative assessment (0-100) measuring a client's preparedness to execute data center development or investment strategies.

## Score Interpretation

### Low Readiness (0-40)
- Minimal industry knowledge
- Unclear capital strategy
- No established relationships
- High education requirement

### Moderate Readiness (41-70)
- Some industry exposure
- Defined capital availability
- Limited operational experience
- Suitable for [[Joint Venture]] partnerships

### High Readiness (71-100)
- Established track record
- Strong capital position
- Operational capabilities
- Ready for [[Greenfield Development]]

## Case Example: Arka Ventures
**Score:** 42/100

**Implications:**
- Early-stage participant
- Requires partner-led strategy ([[Joint Venture]] recommended)
- Should focus on [[Market Education]] and relationship building
- Not ready for independent [[Greenfield Development]]
- Better suited for [[Tier-2 Markets]] or [[Tier-3 Markets]] entry

## Assessment Factors
Typical inputs include:
- Industry experience
- Capital availability and structure
- Technical expertise
- Market relationships
- Regulatory knowledge
- Operational capabilities
