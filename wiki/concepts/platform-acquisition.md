# Platform Acquisition

## Definition

A **platform acquisition** is a private equity strategy where an investor acquires a controlling stake in an established operating company to serve as the foundation ("platform") for future growth, add-on acquisitions, and value creation.

## Key Characteristics

### Established Operations
- Existing revenue streams and customer base
- Proven management team
- Operating infrastructure in place
- Market presence and brand recognition

### Growth Mandate
- Platform serves as base for organic expansion
- Enables bolt-on acquisitions ([[Buy-and-Build Strategy]])
- Operational improvement opportunities
- Geographic or service line expansion

### Control Position
- Typically majority ownership (51%+)
- Board control and governance rights
- Ability to implement strategic initiatives
- Exit optionality through IPO or secondary sale

## Application in Data Centers

In the [[India Data Center Market]], platform acquisitions offer:
- Immediate market entry vs. [[Greenfield Development]]
- Existing permits, land, and infrastructure
- Operational expertise and customer relationships
- Faster path to scale

## Contrast with Other Strategies

- **vs. [[Greenfield Development]]:** Faster, lower execution risk, higher entry cost
- **vs. [[Joint Venture]]:** Full control vs. shared governance
- **vs. Minority Investment:** Control vs. financial participation only
