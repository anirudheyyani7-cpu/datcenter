# Pre-SPAC Co-Investment

A **pre-SPAC co-investment** is equity capital deployed into a private company 12-24 months before a planned [[SPAC merger]], allowing investors to enter at private valuations with near-term public market liquidity.

## Structure

- **Entry Point**: Late-stage private funding round (Series D/E equivalent)
- **Valuation**: 20-40% discount to anticipated SPAC merger valuation
- **Lock-up**: Typically 6-12 months post-merger
- **Upside**: Ratchet provisions, warrants, or preferred terms

## Strategic Rationale

### For Investors

- **Compressed Timeline**: 18-30 month hold vs. 5-7 years for traditional PE
- **Valuation Arbitrage**: Private round pricing + public market re-rating
- **De-Risked Exit**: SPAC merger already negotiated or in process

### For Operators

- **Bridge Capital**: Funds expansion between private and public stages
- **Credible Co-Investors**: Attracts SPAC sponsors, validates business model
- **Balance Sheet Strength**: Improves metrics for PIPE (Private Investment in Public Equity) round

## Example: Yotta Pre-SPAC

[[Blackstone Credit]] targeting $120M for 20-25% stake in [[Yotta Infrastructure]]:

- **Thesis**: Yotta planning SPAC merger within 24 months
- **Entry Valuation**: ~$500M pre-money (implied)
- **SPAC Target**: $1-1.5Bn post-merger valuation
- **Expected Return**: 2-3x in 2 years + ongoing yield from operational assets

## Risks

- SPAC market volatility (redemptions, regulatory changes)
- Execution risk (construction delays, lease-up)
- Lock-up constraints limiting liquidity
