# Hyperscaler BTS Partnership

## Definition
A **Hyperscaler BTS (Build-to-Suit) Partnership** is a contractual arrangement where a [[Data Center Developer]] builds and operates a facility specifically designed for a single [[Hyperscaler]] tenant's requirements.

## Key Characteristics

### Structure
- **Single tenant**: Purpose-built for one [[Cloud Service Provider]]
- **Long-term commitment**: Typically 15-20 year leases
- **Large scale**: Usually 20-100+ MW capacity
- **Custom specifications**: Built to hyperscaler's exact technical requirements

### Typical Deal Flow
1. Hyperscaler issues [[RFP]] or direct engagement
2. Developer proposes [[Greenfield Sites]] with power security
3. Negotiation of [[Build-to-Suit Agreement]] terms
4. Developer secures financing and begins construction
5. Hyperscaler takes occupancy upon [[Tier-3 Certification]]

## Advantages for Developer

### Revenue Security
- Pre-committed long-term revenue stream
- Investment-grade tenant credit
- Predictable cash flows for 15+ years
- Easier project financing

### Market Position
- Instant credibility in [[Data Center Market]]
- Reference customer for future deals
- Relationship with tier-1 [[Hyperscaler]]
- Platform for portfolio expansion

## Entry Barriers

### For New Market Entrants
- **Track record**: Hyperscalers prefer experienced [[DC Developer]] partners
- **Financial strength**: Need significant [[Development Capital]]
- **Technical capability**: Must demonstrate [[Tier-3]] delivery ability
- **Power security**: Critical 60-80 MW allocation required
- **Speed**: Fast site control and permitting essential

## Market Context

### India Opportunity
- $7.5Bn [[India Hyperscale]] market by 2035
- Major metros: [[Mumbai]], [[Chennai]], [[Hyderabad]], [[Pune]]
- Driven by [[AWS India]], [[Microsoft Azure]], [[Google Cloud]] expansion
- Power availability increasingly constrained
