# Build-to-Suit Agreement

## Definition
A **Build-to-Suit (BTS) Agreement** is a contract where a [[Data Center Developer]] commits to construct a facility according to a tenant's specific requirements, with the tenant pre-committing to lease the completed facility.

## Core Components

### Pre-Development Phase
- Site selection and approval by tenant
- Technical specification agreement
- Power and connectivity requirements
- [[Tier-3 Certification]] or higher commitment
- Delivery timeline and milestones

### Commercial Terms
- **Lease duration**: Typically 15-20 years for [[Hyperscaler]] deals
- **Capacity commitment**: Full MW capacity (e.g., 60-80 MW)
- **Pricing structure**: $/kW/month or $/MW/month
- **Escalation**: Annual CPI-linked or fixed increases
- **Service levels**: [[Service Level Agreement]] penalties and credits

### Development Obligations
- Developer finances construction
- Meets technical specifications exactly
- Achieves certification requirements
- Delivers on committed timeline
- Ongoing operations and maintenance

## Risk Allocation

### Developer Risks
- Construction cost overruns
- Permitting and regulatory delays
- [[Power Infrastructure]] procurement
- Technical specification compliance
- Certification achievement

### Tenant Risks
- Long-term capacity commitment
- Market pricing vs. contracted rates
- Single-source dependency
- Geographic concentration

## Financial Implications

### For Developer
- **Capital requirement**: $150-250M for 60-80 MW facility
- **Revenue certainty**: 15-20 year contracted cash flow
- **Financing advantage**: Investment-grade tenant improves debt terms
- **Returns**: 12-18% unlevered IRR typical

### For Tenant
- **Customization**: Purpose-built to exact requirements
- **Speed**: Faster than building internally
- **Off-balance sheet**: Operating lease structure
- **Flexibility**: Can negotiate expansion rights

## Success Factors

### Critical for New Entrants
1. **Site control**: Secure [[Greenfield Sites]] with power
2. **Technical credibility**: Partner with proven [[EPC Contractor]]
3. **Financial backing**: Strong [[Development Capital]] or [[Infrastructure Fund]]
4. **Speed**: Hyperscalers value fast execution
5. **Relationship**: Direct engagement with hyperscaler real estate teams
