# Datacenter Readiness Scoring Framework

## Overview

The **Readiness Score** is a quantitative assessment (0-100) measuring an organization's preparedness to successfully develop and operate datacenter infrastructure.

## Score Ranges & Interpretation

- **80-100**: High readiness - proceed with confidence
- **60-79**: Moderate readiness - targeted improvements needed
- **40-59**: Low readiness - significant preparation required (like [[Notion]] at 42)
- **0-39**: Very low readiness - consider deferring or alternative approaches

## Assessment Dimensions

Typical scoring framework evaluates:

### 1. Technical Capability (25 points)
- In-house engineering expertise
- Understanding of power, cooling, network infrastructure
- Facility management experience
- Vendor relationship maturity

### 2. Financial Capacity (25 points)
- Capital availability and commitment
- Understanding of full lifecycle costs
- Contingency planning
- Access to financing if needed

### 3. Market Knowledge (20 points)
- Understanding of datacenter market dynamics
- Customer/tenant identification
- Competitive landscape awareness
- Regulatory environment familiarity

### 4. Organizational Readiness (15 points)
- Executive commitment and understanding
- Internal stakeholder alignment
- Decision-making capability
- Risk tolerance clarity

### 5. Operational Planning (15 points)
- Staffing strategy
- Training and development plans
- Vendor management approach
- Maintenance and support frameworks

## Score 42 Analysis ([[Notion]] Case)

A score of 42 typically indicates:
- **Financial capacity**: Likely adequate (contributing most points)
- **Technical capability**: Minimal to none
- **Market knowledge**: Basic awareness only
- **Organizational readiness**: Exploratory interest but unclear commitment
- **Operational planning**: Not yet developed

## Improvement Pathways

For clients scoring 40-59:

1. **Education initiatives** - Industry training programs
2. **Partnership strategies** - [[Joint Venture]] or management services
3. **Phased approach** - Start smaller, scale with experience
4. **Expert augmentation** - Hire specialized consultants or interim executives
