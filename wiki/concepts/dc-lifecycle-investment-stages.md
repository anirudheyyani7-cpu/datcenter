# DC Lifecycle Investment Stages

## Overview

Data center investments can be deployed across multiple stages of the asset lifecycle, each with distinct risk/return profiles. Understanding where to invest is critical for institutional capital seeking specific ROI targets.

## Investment Stage Options

### 1. Land Acquisition & Entitlements
**Risk**: Highest | **Return Potential**: 10-20x | **Timeline**: 5-7 years

- Securing [[Power Allocation]] before competitors
- Zoning and environmental approvals
- Infrastructure connectivity (fiber, grid access)
- **Best for**: Patient capital, local market expertise required

### 2. Greenfield Development
**Risk**: High | **Return Potential**: 3-5x | **Timeline**: 3-5 years

- Ground-up construction of [[Tier-3 Data Centers]]
- Design-build control
- Pre-leasing to [[Hyperscale Cloud]] providers
- **Best for**: Developers with execution capability, [[Build-to-Suit]] mandates

### 3. Stabilized Asset Acquisition
**Risk**: Low | **Return Potential**: 1.3-1.8x | **Timeline**: 5-10 years

- Operating facilities with existing tenants
- Predictable cash flows
- Lower execution risk
- **Best for**: Core infrastructure funds, REITs

### 4. Value-Add / Brownfield
**Risk**: Medium | **Return Potential**: 2-3x | **Timeline**: 2-4 years

- Expansion of existing facilities
- Retrofitting for [[AI Infrastructure]] (higher density)
- Lease-up of vacant capacity
- **Best for**: Opportunistic capital, operational expertise

### 5. Power Infrastructure Development
**Risk**: Medium | **Return Potential**: 4-8x | **Timeline**: 3-5 years

- Building substations, [[Captive Power]] plants
- Selling capacity to multiple DC operators
- Creating strategic [[Moat]] through power control
- **Best for**: Utility-adjacent investors, [[India Grid Infrastructure]] specialists

## For 10x Return Targets

Achieving [[10x ROI]] typically requires:

- **Early-stage entry**: Land + power + entitlements
- **Market timing**: Enter before hyperscale demand surge
- **Operational leverage**: Build platform, not single asset
- **Exit strategy**: Sell to core infrastructure buyers or REIT at 4-5% yield
