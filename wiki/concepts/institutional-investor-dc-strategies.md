# Institutional Investor DC Strategies

## Profile: Institutional Capital in Data Centers

Institutional investors like [[Brookfield Asset Management]], [[Blackstone]], and [[DigitalBridge]] treat data centers as core infrastructure assets with predictable cash flows and long-term appreciation potential.

## Strategic Approaches

### Platform Investment
Rather than single assets, institutions build **platforms**:

- Acquire operating team + development pipeline
- $500M-$2B capital commitment
- 5-10 facility portfolio over 3-5 years
- Example: [[Brookfield]]'s approach in India, [[Princeton Digital Group]] in Asia

### Risk/Return Segmentation

**Core (6-8% IRR)**
- Stabilized, leased facilities
- Investment-grade tenants
- Major metros

**Core-Plus (10-12% IRR)**
- Lease-up risk on new builds
- Secondary markets with growth
- Single-tenant [[Hyperscale]] facilities

**Value-Add (15-20% IRR)**
- Development projects
- Market entry plays
- Operational turnarounds

**Opportunistic (20%+ IRR)**
- Greenfield in emerging markets
- Power infrastructure development
- Early-stage platform creation

## The 10x Return Challenge

Achieving [[10x ROI]] in infrastructure is rare and requires:

1. **Market timing**: Enter before asset class is "discovered"
2. **Operational alpha**: Superior execution vs. competition
3. **Multiple expansion**: Buy at development yields, sell at core yields
4. **Leverage**: Use debt intelligently (60-70% LTV typical)
5. **Scale**: Build portfolio, not single asset

### India-Specific Opportunity

The [[India DC Market]] offers 10x potential because:

- Market is early-stage (only ~1,200MW total capacity vs. 10,000MW+ in US)
- [[Hyperscale Cloud]] providers are just entering
- [[Power Availability]] creates supply constraints
- Land + power + execution = significant barriers to entry
- Exit multiples to core buyers can be 12-15x EBITDA

## Competitive Moats

Institutional players win by securing:

- **[[Power Allocation]]** — hardest asset to replicate
- **[[Land Banks]]** near fiber meet points
- **Hyperscale relationships** — pre-commitments de-risk
- **Local partnerships** — [[Joint Venture Structures]] reduce execution risk
