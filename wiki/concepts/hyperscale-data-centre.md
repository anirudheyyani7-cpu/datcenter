# Hyperscale Data Centre Model

## Definition

A **Hyperscale Data Centre** is a large-scale facility designed to support massive computational and storage workloads, typically characterized by:

- **Server Capacity**: Minimum 10,000+ servers
- **Power Capacity**: Minimum 10 MW IT load, typically 50-300+ MW
- **Scalability**: Modular design enabling rapid capacity expansion
- **Efficiency**: Optimized PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) typically below 1.3
- **Automation**: Extensive software-defined infrastructure management
- **Architecture**: Warehouse-scale computing approach

## Scale Characteristics

### Power Profile

**Typical Range**: 50-300+ MW total facility power

**300 MW Example** (as referenced in [[Mytrah Group]] case):

- **IT Load**: ~180-200 MW (assuming 1.4 PUE)
- **Total Power Draw**: 300 MW including cooling and infrastructure
- **Equivalent Capacity**: ~30,000-40,000 server racks
- **Land Requirement**: 15-25 acres minimum
- **Position**: Upper end of single-site deployments
- **Growth Strategy**: Modular/phased capacity expansion

### Space & Density

- **White Space**: 200,000+ sq ft minimum
- **Rack Density**: 10-20 kW per rack (high-density zones)
- **Equivalent Capacity**: ~30,000-40,000 server racks at scale
- **Architecture**: Warehouse-scale computing design

### Infrastructure Requirements

- **Power Redundancy**: N+1 or 2N configuration at scale
- **Cooling Systems**: Direct/indirect evaporative cooling, liquid cooling zones
- **Connectivity**: Multiple fiber entry points, diverse carrier options
- **Security**: Layered physical and cyber security protocols

## Target Customer Segments

### Primary Users

**Hyperscalers**:
- **Cloud Service Providers**: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Alibaba, Oracle Cloud
- **Content Delivery Networks**: Netflix, Meta, content platforms
- **Enterprise Hyperscalers**: Large corporations with massive compute needs
- **AI/ML Workloads**: Training facilities for large language models, deep learning

**Colocation Providers**:
- Equinix, Digital Realty serving enterprise clients

**Large Enterprises**:
- Banking institutions
- Telecommunications companies
- Streaming platforms

### Leasing Models

- **Wholesale**: Multi-megawatt commitments, 10-15 year terms
- **Build-to-Suit**: Custom design for specific tenant requirements
- **Powered Shell**: Tenant completes fit-out to specifications
- **Speculative Development**: Pre-built capacity for future tenants

## Financial Considerations

### Capital Investment

**Per MW Development Cost**: $8-15 million USD

- **300 MW Facility**: $2.4-4.5 billion total investment
- **Construction Timeline**: 24-36 months
- **Model Type**: CapEx-intensive with long-term returns
- **Lease Commitments**: 10-15 year terms typical for revenue stability

### Development Strategies

- **Build-to-Suit**: Pre-committed tenant funding
- **Speculative Development**: Developer-financed capacity
- **[[Partnership Strategy]]**: Often essential for first-time developers

## Greenfield Considerations

For [[Greenfield Data Centre Development]]:

### Critical Site Selection Factors
- **Power Availability**: Reliable grid capacity and redundancy
- **Fiber Connectivity**: Multiple diverse carrier access points
- **Geographic Location**: Latency, climate, disaster risk zones
- **Land**: 15-25+ acres for full build-out

### Development Challenges
- **Regulatory Approvals**: Environmental, zoning processes (lengthy)
- **Timeline**: 24-36 months typical construction
- **Utility Coordination**: Grid upgrades, substation capacity
- **Risk Mitigation**: [[Partnership Strategy]] recommended for new entrants
