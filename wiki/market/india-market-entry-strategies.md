# India Market Entry Strategies

**Context:** [[India Data Center Market]] ($6.4B by 2030)
**Investor Profile:** International PE firms, institutional capital

## Primary Entry Pathways

### 1. Platform Acquisition (Control)

**Structure:** Acquire 51-100% of existing operator

**Advantages:**
- Immediate market presence
- Existing customer contracts and revenue
- Operating team and expertise in place
- Permits, land, power allocations secured

**Considerations:**
- Higher entry valuation
- Integration and operational control
- Due diligence on legacy infrastructure

**Best For:** Investors like [[Blackstone Capital]] seeking immediate scale

### 2. Joint Venture

**Structure:** 50/50 or minority stake with local partner

**Advantages:**
- Local knowledge and government relationships
- Shared capital requirements
- Lower political/regulatory risk
- Access to land and power through partner

**Considerations:**
- Governance complexity
- Alignment on strategy and exit
- Partner selection critical

**Best For:** First-time entrants, regulatory-sensitive sectors

### 3. Greenfield Development

**Structure:** Build new facilities from ground up

**Advantages:**
- Custom design for target customers
- Modern infrastructure and efficiency
- Full control over specifications
- Potentially lower cost per MW in [[Tier-2 Cities]]

**Considerations:**
- 18-24 month construction timeline
- Permitting and land acquisition risk
- Pre-leasing requirements for debt financing
- Execution capability required

**Best For:** Patient capital, build-to-core strategies

### 4. Brownfield Expansion Capital

**Structure:** Growth equity in existing operator

**Advantages:**
- Lower entry valuation vs. control
- Proven operator with expansion pipeline
- Co-investment with management

**Considerations:**
- Limited governance rights
- Dependent on operator execution
- Exit alignment with majority holder

## Geographic Strategy Options

- **Pan-India Platform:** Presence across top 4-5 cities
- **Metro Focus:** Deep position in [[Mumbai]], [[Delhi NCR]], [[Bangalore]]
- **Tier-2/3 Strategy:** Early mover in emerging markets
