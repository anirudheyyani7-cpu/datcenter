# India Data Center Market Overview

India represents one of the world's fastest-growing data center markets, driven by digital transformation, cloud adoption, data localization regulations, and massive digital adoption across a 1.4 billion population.

## Market Size & Growth

- **2025 Projection**: $10 billion market size
- **2030 Projection**: $22 billion market size
- **Investment Capital**: ~$15 billion in private equity and infrastructure capital deployment targeting India data centers by 2030
- **CAGR**: ~17% (2025-2030)
- **Current Capacity**: ~850-950MW (estimated)
- **Projected Capacity Need**: 2,000+ MW by 2030
- **Market Condition**: Demand consistently outpaces supply, particularly for Tier-3+ facilities with high power density

## Key Demand Drivers

### 1. Cloud Provider Expansion
- [[AWS]], [[Microsoft Azure]], [[Google Cloud]] establishing regional presence and Indian regions
- Data sovereignty requirements driving local infrastructure
- [[Hyperscale DC]] deployments in Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad
- Global cloud providers requiring multi-hundred MW facilities to serve local and regional customers

### 2. Digital India Initiatives
- Government digitization programs promoting digital services, financial inclusion, and smart cities
- UPI and fintech infrastructure growth
- Smart city projects requiring [[Edge Data Centers]]
- Massive data generation and storage requirements

### 3. Data Sovereignty and Localization
- Increasing regulatory requirements for local data storage
- Compliance needs in financial services, healthcare, and government sectors
- Data localization regulations driving domestic infrastructure investment

### 4. Content & Streaming
- OTT platform growth requiring CDN infrastructure
- Gaming and esports expansion
- Social media localization

### 5. Emerging Technologies
- AI and ML workload growth
- 5G network rollout driving edge computing demand

## Market Dynamics

### Supply-Demand Imbalance
Despite rapid capacity additions, the market experiences consistent undersupply, creating favorable conditions for developers and operators with quality assets.

### Institutional Capital Interest
Significant private equity and infrastructure fund activity reflects confidence in long-term fundamentals and growth trajectory of the Indian digital economy.
