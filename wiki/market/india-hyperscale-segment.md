# India Hyperscale Data Center Market

## Market Size & Growth

### Current Opportunity
- **Market value**: $7.5 Billion by 2035
- **Segment**: Hyperscale segment alone (excludes retail/enterprise)
- **Growth drivers**: Cloud adoption, digital transformation, data localization
- **CAGR**: Estimated 15-20% through 2035

## Key Market Dynamics

### Demand Drivers
1. **Cloud Service Provider Expansion**
   - [[AWS India]] aggressive growth
   - [[Microsoft Azure]] availability zone expansion
   - [[Google Cloud]] regional presence
   - [[Oracle Cloud]] emerging requirements

2. **Regulatory Push**
   - Data localization requirements
   - GDPR-equivalent privacy laws
   - Government digital initiatives
   - Banking and financial sector mandates

3. **Digital Transformation**
   - Enterprise cloud migration
   - 5G rollout requirements
   - IoT and edge computing growth
   - AI/ML workload expansion

## Geographic Concentration

### Primary Markets
1. **[[Mumbai]]**
   - Financial services hub
   - Submarine cable landing station
   - Largest existing DC capacity
   - Power constraints emerging

2. **[[Chennai]]**
   - Submarine cable connectivity
   - Lower power costs
   - Manufacturing and IT hub
   - Growing hyperscaler presence

3. **[[Hyderabad]]**
   - IT services concentration
   - Government support
   - Competitive power rates
   - Emerging hyperscaler location

4. **[[Pune]]**
   - Proximity to Mumbai
   - Manufacturing base
   - Lower land costs
   - Disaster recovery location

## Supply-Side Constraints

### Critical Challenges
1. **[[Power Infrastructure]]**
   - 60-80 MW allocations increasingly scarce
   - Grid capacity limitations in metros
   - Regulatory approval timelines
   - Rising power costs

2. **Land Availability**
   - [[Greenfield Sites]] with power access limited
   - Zoning and permitting complexity
   - Rising land prices in key metros

3. **Skilled Labor**
   - [[DC Operations]] talent shortage
   - Certified technicians limited
   - Competition for experienced teams
