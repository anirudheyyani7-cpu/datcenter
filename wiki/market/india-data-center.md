# India Data Center Market

India is experiencing explosive data center growth driven by [[Digital India]], cloud adoption, and local data sovereignty regulations.

## Market Size

- **Current Capacity**: ~800MW operational (2024)
- **Pipeline**: 1,200MW under construction
- **Projected Growth**: $22Bn total investment by 2030 (per [[Blackstone Credit]] funnel)

## Key Metros

### Tier-1 Markets

- **[[Mumbai]]**: Subsea cable landing hub, 60% of existing capacity, land-constrained
- **[[Chennai]]**: Emerging hyperscale cluster, lower power costs, coastal cooling advantage
- **[[Hyderabad]]**: Tech talent hub, government incentives, [[Hiranandani]] developments
- **[[Pune]]**: Enterprise market, secondary overflow from Mumbai

### Tier-2 Expansion

- [[Bangalore]] (power constraints)
- [[Noida]]/NCR (regulatory complexity)
- [[Kolkata]] (emerging)

## Demand Drivers

1. **Cloud Providers**: [[AWS]] (3 regions), [[Azure]] (3 regions), [[GCP]] (2 regions + expansion)
2. **OTT/Streaming**: [[Netflix]], [[Hotstar]], [[Amazon Prime Video]]
3. **Financial Services**: RBI data localization mandates
4. **AI/ML**: Growing GPU demand for domestic AI startups

## Supply Constraints

- **Power**: Grid capacity maxed in Mumbai (15-18 month lead times for new substations)
- **Land**: Tier-1 metro real estate at premium ($200-400/sq ft)
- **Skilled Labor**: DC operations talent shortage

## Investment Trends

- **Institutional Capital**: [[Blackstone Credit]], DigitalBridge, Brookfield entering market
- **REIT Pathway**: [[Embassy]] and [[Mindspace]] exploring DC asset additions
- **Greenfield Development**: 18-24 month build timelines for [[Tier-3]] facilities

## Regulatory Environment

- **DESH Bill**: Proposed data embassy zones with tax incentives
- **Power Tariffs**: Industrial rates 7-12 ₹/kWh depending on state
- **FDI**: 100% allowed under automatic route
