# Data Center Market Outlook - 2030

## Market Context
The 2030 timeframe represents a critical planning horizon for [[Data Center Development]]. Firms like [[Arka Ventures]] are targeting $22 billion in development pipeline by this date, reflecting broader market growth expectations.

## Growth Drivers to 2030

### AI and Machine Learning
- [[GPU Clusters]] requiring massive power density
- [[AI Training Facilities]] concentrated in specific markets
- Exponential compute demand from foundation models
- Edge deployment for [[AI Inference]] workloads

### Cloud Expansion
- Continued [[Hyperscale]] buildout by major providers
- Geographic diversification for latency and resilience
- Sovereign cloud requirements driving regional development

### Power Infrastructure Constraints
- Competition for available utility capacity
- [[Greenfield Development]] in power-rich regions
- On-site generation and [[Renewable Energy]] integration
- [[Nuclear Power]] emerging for large campus developments

## Investment Volume Projections
With individual firms targeting multi-billion dollar pipelines:
- Total market investment likely exceeds $500 billion globally
- North America remaining dominant but international growth accelerating
- [[Tier-3 Markets]] capturing increasing share of deployments

## Market Structure Evolution

### Geographic Shifts
- Power availability driving [[Site Selection]] over traditional factors
- Secondary markets becoming primary due to constraints
- International markets gaining competitiveness

### Development Models
- [[Build-to-Suit]] remaining dominant for large deployments
- [[JV Partnerships]] increasing as capital requirements grow
- Vertical integration as hyperscalers develop directly

## Implications for Market Entrants
- Early power securing critical for pipeline development
- Partnership speed more important than perfect deal structure
- Geographic flexibility essential for meeting volume targets
- Technical expertise (especially power) increasingly valuable
